About

Born and raised in Iowa, Bob Davis found his home in Texas in 2017 and never looked back. He writes honest, road-worn country music built from real events rather than assembled from parts. After a 14-year career in software, he traded the keyboard for a different kind of instrument. He placed third for Male Songwriter of the Year at the 2026 Texas State Songwriters Association Awards, qualifying for the statewide Songwriter of the Year competition in January 2027. His debut single, 2 Dolla Bill, arrives October 2.
